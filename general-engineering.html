Custom Fabrication

Tasman Engineering runs a full custom fabrication shop, building everything from small components to large assemblies. Our qualified welders and fabricators work to your drawings, samples or concept, whether you need a single one-off or a full production run.

If You Can Draw It, We Can Build It

Our team is experienced in both light and heavy fabrication, and we pride ourselves on the variety of materials and sizes we can handle. We work closely with you from the first conversation to the finished product, with design, tooling and fabrication all under one roof.

What we fabricate:

  • One-off and prototype builds
  • Repeat and production runs of welded product
  • Small components through to large assemblies
  • Custom jigs and fixtures, so repeat jobs stay consistent and cost-effective

Welding and fabrication capability:

  • MIG welding
  • TIG welding
  • Light fabrication
  • Heavy fabrication
  • Jig and fixture design and build

Materials: Steel, stainless steel, copper, aluminium and more.

Finishing: Hot dip galvanising, zinc plating, powder coating, painting.
