About Us

For over 40 years, Tasman Engineering has helped customers turn concepts into finished products. From design and prototyping through to full production, we do it all in house, so you deal with one team and one point of contact from start to finish.

Professional Workers

Our qualified engineers and welders are experienced in both light and heavy fabrication. They work closely with you to understand your goals, and treat every job, one-off or production run, as a priority.

  • Qualified engineers, welders and machinists
  • Experience across a wide range of materials and sizes
  • In-house design team to solve problems before they reach the shop floor

Advanced Technology

We invest in the equipment that makes your parts better and your costs lower.

  • Modern CNC turning and machining equipment
  • Ten mechanical power presses from 30 to 200 tonnes, with automated coil feed and air cushions for drawing
  • CAD/CAM capability for efficient programming and tooling
  • Machining and pressing practices that deliver real per-part savings

Quality Products

From simple stock items like galvanised U-bolts and plates through to complex components and assemblies, we deliver a full service from design to production, on time.

  • Consistent quality across prototype, one-off and production runs
  • Hot dip galvanised stock products for maximum protection
  • Spot-on deadlines you can plan around

One Supplier, Start to Finish

If you have a complex component or assembly, you shouldn't have to coordinate several workshops. Tasman brings together:

  • Design & Development: concept, drawings and prototyping
  • Press Services: tooling and production pressing
  • CNC & Precision Engineering: turning, milling and machining
  • Custom Fabrication: welding, jigs and fixtures, light and heavy builds
  • Stock products: U-bolts, U-brackets and plates, ready to go

Whether you need low-cost, high-volume product or low-volume, high-value work, we'll help you get there.
