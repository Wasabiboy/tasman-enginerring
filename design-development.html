Design & Development

In-House Design & Development

Our in-house design team draws on a wealth of experience and will provide a depth of expertise in finding solutions for a diverse range of components, equipment and machinery. CAD facilities streamline the design process and give us the ability to create products from the customers own specifications.

We have a varied and sophisticated range of engineering capabilities. With our total design and manufacture service we'll produce products using Stainless Steel, Brass, Copper, Mild and Tensile Steels, Aluminium, Nylon and many other Plastics.

Our Design Capabilities

  • CAD/CAM design services
  • Product development from concept to production
  • Prototype development
  • Custom design solutions
  • Working with customer specifications
  • Multi-material expertise

From initial concept through to final production, our design team works closely with you to ensure your vision becomes reality. We combine traditional engineering expertise with modern CAD technology to deliver innovative solutions.

Tasman Engineering provides complete engineering solutions. From design to prototyping, then full production - we can do it all in house.
