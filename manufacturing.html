Press Services & CNC Precision Engineering

Whether you need low-cost, high-volume parts or low-volume, high-value components, Tasman Engineering can take your concept from design through prototyping to full production, all in house. Two of our core capabilities are our press plant and our CNC machining operation.

Press Services

Pressed to Spec. Built to Volume.

Our press plant produces components in steel, stainless steel, copper and aluminium. We develop products from prototype and production tooling through to the finished part, so the whole process stays under one roof with one point of contact.

Our press capability:

  • Ten mechanical power presses, with capacity from 30 to 200 tonnes
  • Several presses with very large platen dimensions, for pressing larger components
  • Automated coil feed for efficient, repeatable production runs
  • Air cushions for drawing operations
  • In-house prototype and production tooling

Materials: Steel, stainless steel, copper, aluminium

Why press with Tasman:

  • Prototype to production: Tooling, prototyping and production run in house, so there are fewer hand-offs and less lead time lost between suppliers.
  • Range of tonnage: From small precision parts at 30 tonnes up to heavy components at 200 tonnes.
  • Suited to volume: Coil feed automation keeps repeat runs consistent and cost-effective.
  • One supplier: Pressing, machining and fabrication can be combined on a single assembly, so you don't have to coordinate several workshops.

CNC & Precision Engineering

Precision Parts. Delivered on Time.

Our precision turning and machining operation produces a comprehensive range of components on modern CNC equipment. Using efficient machining practices, we deliver consistent quality and real per-part savings, from one-off prototypes to repeat production runs.

What we offer:

  • CAD/CAM programming
  • Precision machining
  • CNC turning
  • CNC milling
  • 2, 3 and 4-axis machining
  • Bar-fed components
  • Machining from billets and castings

Why machine with Tasman:

  • Design support in house: CAD/CAM and our design team mean we can refine a part for manufacture before cutting begins.
  • Per-part savings: We plan machining to cut cycle times and cost, which matters most on repeat orders.
  • One supplier: Pressing, machining and fabrication can be combined on a single assembly, so you don't have to coordinate several workshops.
